Privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Cursus is a mobile game. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and how you can delete it.

What we use

  • Your game. Your progress (messengers, deliveries, coins, achievements) is stored on your phone and, when online, in Google Firebase (Firestore), tied to an anonymous identifier. We don't ask for your real name, email or phone number. If you link your Google or Apple account, we keep that link so you can recover your game.
  • Usage statistics. If you allow it (Settings → Usage data), the game sends anonymous events to Google Analytics for Firebase: which screens and routes you use and when you come back. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Crashes. If the game crashes, a technical report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it.
  • Ads. Ads are optional (only when you tap "Watch ad"). They are served by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers according to the consent you give. In the European Economic Area and the UK we ask for that consent at start; you can change it in Settings.
  • Purchases. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store payment details; only the transaction ID, so it is never credited twice.

Why

To run the game, save your progress, understand what to improve and show ads when you ask for them. We don't sell your data or use it to track you across apps.

How long

As long as you have a game. If you delete your account, your cloud save and identifier are deleted.

How to delete your data

In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account and data. It deletes your game on the phone and in the cloud, and the account. You can also write to CONTACTO@COMPLETAR.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Third-party services

Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), Google AdMob, the Apple App Store and Google Play. Each has its own privacy policy.
